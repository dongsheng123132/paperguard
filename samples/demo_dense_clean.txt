Dense Clean Paper: Realistic Statistical Reporting

This paper presents a 2x2 factorial trial of attention training in adults.

Participants and Design
Sixty adults (N = 60, 30 per condition) completed pre/post tests.

Demographics
- Age (continuous): mean = 24.50 (SD = 3.10, N = 60). Note: continuous variable, GRIM not applicable.
- Years of education: mean = 16.20 (SD = 2.40, N = 60). Continuous variable.
- Baseline working memory: mean = 7.20 (SD = 1.50, N = 60). Continuous variable.

Pre-test Outcomes
- Group A pretest mean = 50.10 (SD = 8.20, N = 30) -> 1503 / 30 = 50.1, OK
- Group B pretest mean = 51.30 (SD = 7.50, N = 30) -> 1539 / 30 = 51.3, OK

Inferential Statistics (all internally consistent)
Pretest groups did not differ, t(58) = 0.62, p = 0.538.
Main effect of training was significant, F(1, 56) = 12.34, p = 0.001.
Time x Group interaction, F(1, 56) = 5.67, p = 0.021.
Correlation between baseline and improvement, r(58) = 0.38, p = 0.003.
Categorical handedness balance: chi2(1) = 0.04, p = 0.842.

Notes on numerical conventions
Some numbers like 51.30 may repeat across rows because both groups have similar means;
this is normal experimental data and should NOT trigger PaperGuard. The detector should
distinguish between "shared numbers due to similar measurements" and "verbatim copy-paste".
